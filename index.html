BiHOI dataset

1,575 bimanual sequences. Captured from 32 views.

32 calibrated RGB cameras surround a tabletop workspace and record at 1920 × 1232 pixels and 30 fps. We reconstruct both hands and the manipulated object, then retarget the motion to robot hands.

Generalization

New objects. No fine-tuning.

Held-out objects from public datasets, from the human recording to the learned bimanual execution. Shorter source clips hold their final frame.

Method

One shared policy. Verified demonstrations in the loop.

A

Object-centered per-hand reward

Object tracking stays active throughout the motion, while each hand is supervised by the contacts its reference demands, so one hand's progress is not masked by the other.

B

Clip-Centered Advantages

Subtracting each clip's mean estimated advantage before the PPO update removes reference-dependent offsets while keeping the differences within a clip.

C

PPO + behavior cloning

Successful executions are replay-verified and recycled into a demonstration library that supplies behavior-cloning targets in later updates.
